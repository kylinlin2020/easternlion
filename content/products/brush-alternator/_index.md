The STC series brush alternator is a synchronous generator with a salient-pole rotor and brush excitation. It is a proven, cost-effective solution for standby power, agricultural machinery, construction sites and small industrial plants.

Standard output ranges from 8 kW to 500 kW at 50 Hz, with 60 Hz ratings available on request. Single-phase and three-phase versions, SAE couplings and custom voltages can be supplied.
