Every generator set is the same machine with a different job in front of it. The job decides the duty, the enclosure, the fuel autonomy, the control system and the way the set is tested before it ships. Start from the application you are building for, check the typical load against the sizing table, and the series pages take you to the exact model with its engine, alternator and controller data.
