Fuan Eastern Lion Electric Machines Co., Ltd is a professional manufacturer of generators and diesel generator sets, with a history of more than 30 years of development. The company has production bases in Fujian, Jiangsu, Shanghai and other cities, and markets its products throughout China and around the world.

Eastern Lion has an experienced technical team, implementing modern management, establishing a complete quality system and supporting high-precision, large-capacity testing equipment. Raw materials, production and testing are strictly controlled to ensure the quality of every product.

Diesel generator sets are made with famous-brand Cummins, Perkins and Yuchai engines for the domestic market and abroad. The generator itself is independently researched and produced by Eastern Lion, or equipped with Stamford, Marathon, Engga, Leroy-Somer and other well-known domestic and international brands. Product specifications range from 2 kW to 2000 kW and are widely used in high-rise civil buildings, enterprises, railways, construction sites, telecommunications, military and oil-field applications.

## Product inspection

- Generator set inspection
- Electric leakage test
- Rotor detection
- Silence detection
- Voltage capacity test

Environmental protection and energy saving are the new trend of the generator industry, and Eastern Lion always adheres to that principle in product selection. The company strictly controls the three environmental indicators of noise, emissions and fuel consumption, and continuously improves the performance of diesel engines, alternators and sound-absorbing materials. Stable operation of power, current, frequency and speed is verified for every unit. Production also applies strict and effective testing methods such as rotor dynamic balance, pressure resistance, resistance value and whole-machine testing.
