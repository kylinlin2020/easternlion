<div class="timeline">
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">1987</div>
    <p>Hanyang electric plant was founded, initiating the motor rotor extrusion process.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">1995</div>
    <p>Researched and developed the ST-STC series 6.5 kW &ndash; 30 kW small generator.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2000</div>
    <p>Researched and developed the TZH series phase-compound excitation system generator.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2003</div>
    <p>Based on the philosophy of &ldquo;stable quality, never engage in false&rdquo;, laid a good foundation in the national market when the generator market was expanding rapidly.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2006</div>
    <p>Invested 50 million yuan to build a modern standard workshop covering an area of 6,000 square meters.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2007</div>
    <p>Introduced the Jiangsu technical team to research and develop brushless generators and diesel generator sets. The first vacuum insulation coating equipment in Fu'an was introduced to improve product service life.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2008</div>
    <p>Sales reached 150 million yuan and the company entered the top five in the Chinese market, setting up 35 sales and service offices in Hong Kong, Shanghai, Guangdong, Jiangsu and other places.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2009</div>
    <p>Increased capital investment to create unique testing equipment, ensuring that all products are qualified before leaving the factory.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2010</div>
    <p>The diesel generator set assembly factory was established in Jiangsu. In the same year, diesel generators became designated cooperative products of the Zhejiang Wenzhou education system.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2011</div>
    <p>After industrial technology adjustment, annual production capacity reached 2,000 generator sets and 18,000 alternators.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2012</div>
    <p>The Shanghai branch of the company was established, with generator production and sales as its direct business.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2016</div>
    <p>Eastern Lion Electric Machines Co., Ltd. was established and a foreign trade team was set up to explore the international market. Within one month, the first 100 kW diesel generator was exported to the British market with high customer satisfaction.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="year">2017</div>
    <p>Yangzhou Lion Power Equipment Co., Ltd. was established. Global marketing expanded, with products exported to Russia, Indonesia, the Philippines, South Africa, Pakistan, the United Kingdom and other countries.</p>
  </div>
</div>
