## How to choose a series

Every generator set order comes down to six decisions: the engine brand (it decides the spare
parts and the response time of your service partner), prime or standby duty (prime runs 8 to 12
hours a day, standby is for emergency backup; size the set so the load sits at 70 to 80 % of the
standby rating), voltage and frequency (400/230 V at 50 Hz, 230/400 V at 60 Hz), the enclosure
(open frame, silent canopy, container, trailer), starting and transfer (electric start with an
automatic transfer switch for standby sets) and paralleling (more than one set needs a power
management system). Site conditions matter too: every 1000 m of altitude and every 10 °C above
the standard ambient costs about 3 % of the rating.

Warranty: 1 year or 1000 hours. Minimum order: 1 set. Enclosure options: open frame, silent
canopy, trailer and container. Starting: electric, with an ATS on request. Parallel systems with
power management are available.
