# 东方电机 — 福建东方电机股份有限公司

> 福建东方电机股份有限公司（品牌：东方电机 / Eastern Lion）是中国的柴油发电机组、有刷交流发电机与无刷交流发电机专业生产厂家，1987 年创立于福建福安。公司自有车间完成发电机绕制、发电机组装配与满载测试，产品销往全球 40 多个国家和地区。功率范围 2kW – 2000kW，提供 50Hz 与 60Hz 两种频率，可选康明斯、珀金斯、玉柴、玉动、潍柴、科发、华柴、龙信、卧龙、扬动、云内、玉兴、凯迅等发动机。

## 本站内容

- 44 个产品系列，每一系列都有完整规格表（型号、额定/备用功率、发动机、发电机、控制器）并可下载 CSV

# Eastern Lion — Fuan Eastern Lion Electric Machines Co., Ltd

> Fuan Eastern Lion Electric Machines Co., Ltd (brand: Eastern Lion, 福建东方电机) is a Chinese
> manufacturer of diesel generator sets, brush alternators and brushless alternators, founded in
> 1987 in Fuan, Fujian. It builds alternators and complete generator sets in its own workshops,
> load tests every machine before shipment, and sells them worldwide — 2 kW to 2000 kW, 50 Hz and
> 60 Hz, with Cummins, Perkins, Yuchai, YTO, Weichai, Kofo, Huachai, Longxin, Lovol, Woling,
> Yangdong, Yunnei, Yuxing and Kaixun engine options.

## What this site is

- 44 product series, each with a full specification table (model, prime and standby power,
  engine, alternator, controller) and a downloadable csv
- The power finder matches a load in kW to every model that covers it
- The comparison tool puts up to four series side by side
- Every specification list is available as csv at /download/
